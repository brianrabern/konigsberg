# KostochkaCliqueGraph

## Book
`\label{KostochkaCliqueGraph}`: If 𝒬 is a collection of maximum cliques with
ω(G) > (2/3)(Δ(G)+1) and X_𝒬 connected, then ⋂𝒬 ≠ ∅.

## Fidelity review
Strict inequality ω > (2/3)(Δ+1) encoded as `2(Δ+1) < 3ω` in ℕ.
`cliqueIntersectionGraph 𝒬` is X_𝒬; conclusion is nonempty intersection of
the cliques as sets.
